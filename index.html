Briefly, on full stack

What it really means.

What is a full stack developer?

Someone who can take a feature from idea to production across the interface, the server, the data and the deployment. Not an expert in everything, but fluent enough to make good calls at every layer.

When does it pay off?

Early products, small teams and anything where speed of learning matters. One person holding the whole picture cuts meetings, ambiguity and the gap between design and reality.

Where does it bite?

At extreme scale or deep specialisation, such as database internals or graphics engines. Knowing when to call a specialist is part of the skill.

How does AI fit in?

I work as an AI-assisted developer. Claude and ChatGPT are my pair programmers for drafts and exploration, while I own the architecture, the review and the security decisions.

My rule of thumb

Go deep on one layer, stay dangerous on the rest. Ship the thinnest vertical slice first, then thicken it.
